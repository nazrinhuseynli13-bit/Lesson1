Popularly known as the "Father of the Digital World",Steve Jobs was an American entrepreneur,investor,and co-founder of Apple Inc.He helped transform the world of consumer electronics with his innovations in music and mobile phones. The founder of Apple Inc.,Pixar Animation Studios and Next Inc.,Jobs gave information technology his life and blood. A master of innovation,he was known for this perfectionist attitude and futuristic ideas. During his years at Apple,he helped develop the iMac,iTunes,iPod,iPhone and iPad.
